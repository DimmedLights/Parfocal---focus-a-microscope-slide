Every clip in `videos/` is real microscopy footage from Wikimedia Commons (the media library behind Wikipedia), cropped, trimmed and re-encoded as a seamless loop (480×480 H.264, no audio, 20–40 s, crossfaded at the seam). In play it is blurred, sectioned and filtered by the game. Clips under CC BY-SA are shared under the same licence.
